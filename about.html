Our Story

Bringing Fresh Groceries Closer to You

Nana Mini Mart started as a small neighborhood store with one simple goal: make everyday shopping easier, faster and more affordable for local families. What began as a single shop has grown into a trusted name for fresh groceries, daily essentials and quality products.

Today, we combine that same neighborhood-store warmth with a modern online shopping experience — so you can browse, order, and get your essentials delivered without ever leaving home.

Our Mission

To deliver fresh, high-quality groceries and daily essentials at fair prices, with a shopping experience that's simple, fast and reliable for every customer.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted mini mart for every household in our community — known for freshness, fair pricing and genuine care for our customers.
